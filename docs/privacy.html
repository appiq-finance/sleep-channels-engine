Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy covers the "Sleep Channels Engine" application operated by AppIQ (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa, and this website.

What the application does

Sleep Channels Engine is a private publishing tool. It is used only by AppIQ (Pty) Ltd to upload, schedule and manage videos, playlists and comments on its own YouTube channels (Stormwood Cabin and Valley & Tide) and to read those channels' own analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channels you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Information we access and store

  • The OAuth access granted by the channel owner, stored encrypted and used only to publish to and manage our own channels.
  • Our channels' own video metadata and aggregated analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, country mix).
  • Public comments posted on our own videos, read so we can reply and remove spam. We do not sell, share or use commenters' information for any other purpose, and we do not build profiles of viewers.

This website

This website does not use cookies, trackers or analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

Retention and deletion

Analytics summaries are kept for as long as the channels operate. Stored access is deleted when the application stops being used or when access is revoked.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the About page of either YouTube channel.
